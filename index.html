How it works

  • Duration: Oct 30, 12:00 AM to Nov 6, 10:00 PM (PH time). Winners announced Nov 7.
  • TV formula: Position size × Leverage × 2 (open + close). Example: 100 USDT at 20x opened and closed = 4,000 TV.
  • Starting capital: Max 200 USDT in your futures account at the start. No top-ups or transfers in during the week. You can keep trading with your profits.
  • Qualify: Reach at least 1,000,000 USDT TV within the week. Highest TV among qualifiers wins.
  • What counts: Crypto perpetual futures on your own BingX account. Not counted: spot, CFDs (stocks, forex, commodities), bots, and referral or community volume.
  • Tie-breaker: Higher net realized PnL in the window, then earliest registration.
  • Fair play: 1 UID per KOL. No sub or alt accounts, no wash trading. BingX PH may disqualify any account, and its decision is final.
